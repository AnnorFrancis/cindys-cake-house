Delivery & collection

We deliver every day (until 15:00 on Mondays) in our own air-conditioned van, in two-hour windows you choose at checkout. Fees depend on your area, from free in Cantonments to GHS 250 for Aburi and Peduase. The full list is on the home page.

Tiered cakes and dessert tables are delivered by two of our team and set up at the venue for GHS 450 within Accra. Please make sure there is a level, shaded table ready, and someone to receive us.

Collections are from 12 Nii Sai Road, Cantonments, during opening hours. Counter orders placed before noon can be delivered the same day.

Orders & deposits

Cakes, tables and tastings are confirmed with a 50% deposit by Mobile Money or card. The balance is due 72 hours before delivery or collection. Counter orders are paid in full.

Cancel up to 7 days before and we refund the deposit in full. Within 7 days we keep the deposit, because ingredients are bought and sugar flowers are made. We're always happy to move the date instead, if the kitchen has room.

Every cake is made by hand, so small differences from photos are part of the craft. We never copy another artist's design exactly.

Caring for your cake

Keep buttercream cakes in a cool room, out of the sun and away from the generator. Cut within six hours of delivery, or refrigerate and bring back to room temperature for an hour before serving. Fresh flowers are decorative: remove them before cutting.

Privacy

We keep your name, phone, email and address to make and deliver your order. If you ask us to remind you next year, we also keep the occasion and date, and send one WhatsApp message about three weeks before. Reply STOP and we delete it.

Inspiration photos you upload are seen only by our team and deleted 90 days after your order. We don't sell or share your details, and card payments are handled by our payment partner, never on this site.
