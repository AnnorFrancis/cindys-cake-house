Our story

Baked here,|for here

We bake with what Ghana grows best: cocoa from one Ashanti cooperative, mangoes and passion fruit from the Volta, flowers from a grower in Aburi. The butter is cultured and the patience is local.

  1. 2016One oven in a borrowed kitchen. Twelve birthday cakes in the first month, all for friends of friends.
  2. 2018The first wedding: five tiers for 220 guests in Peduase. Nana Ama joins to make the sugar flowers.
  3. 2021The house on Nii Sai Road, with a pâtisserie counter and a garden table for tastings.
  4. 2024Efua takes over the morning lamination. Croissants by 7:10, six mornings a week.
  5. 2026The Atelier goes online: design, price and book a cake in one sitting.

What goes in

Four things|we won't compromise

Ashanti cocoa

Single-origin, from one cooperative near Konongo. In every ganache, truffle and chocolate sponge.

Volta fruit

Mangoes, passion fruit and pineapple, bought in season and turned into curds and glazes the same week.

Cultured butter

Three days of lamination for every croissant. No margarine, no shortcuts, no exceptions.

Aburi flowers

Fresh roses and greenery from a grower in the hills, pesticide-free and wired with food-safe tape.
